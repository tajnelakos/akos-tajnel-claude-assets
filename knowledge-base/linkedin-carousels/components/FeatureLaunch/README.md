# FeatureLaunch

Launches a capability: a "New in …" cover, the problem it solves, one or two screenshots on paper, and a close.

**Use it for** product news that existing customers can use now and prospects can see in a demo. One capability per carousel, shown rather than described. 4–6 slides.

**You provide**
- The feature or area name, literal and capitalised (Evidence Trail, Coverage Map), for the cover: "New in Evidence Trail".
- One `body-lead` line that says what the reader can now do ("Every moment now opens with a one-line reason your advisors can say out loud.").
- The problem in one statement, from the user's side ("Advisors read four data points, then improvise the opening line.").
- One or two real screenshots, cropped to the one panel that proves the point, with illustrative data and customer IDs only.
- A benefit heading for each screenshot, in the reader's words.
- Availability in plain words for the close ("Available now in Evidence Trail, at no extra cost."), checked with product marketing.

**Build rules**
- Cover: lockup top-left, `ridge-lines` motif top-right, `title-cover` "New in [feature]" plus one `body-lead` line, anchored 240px from the bottom. Arrow at the margin.
- Problem: navy, eyebrow "The problem", `title-statement` with up to 3 words in `gold-light`.
- Screenshots: paper slides. Eyebrow = the feature name; `heading` = the benefit; frame at the margins from y 392px, 690–760px tall, so the footer row stays clear. One gold element per slide: either a reason rule (6px gold left border on the new element) or the top weight bar, never both. Use skeleton bars for anything that isn't the point.
- Close: `title-cover` "See a sample moment." (or the right action) plus availability, lockup centred at the bottom.

**Don't**
- Don't invent a feature name, a ™ or a cute brand name ("TraceIQ"). Literal names only.
- Don't use a generic UI illustration when a real screen exists, or more than one screenshot per slide.
- Don't let a screenshot run under the counter, arrow and mark.
- Don't promise a date, price or tier without product marketing's confirmation.
