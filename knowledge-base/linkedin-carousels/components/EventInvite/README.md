# EventInvite

An invitation to meet the team or hear a talk: a centred question, the talk, the speaker, then date, place and one button.

**Use it for** conferences, stands, webinars and roundtables. The question earns the swipe; the details close it. 4–5 slides.

**You provide**
- A cover question for one persona's situation, the topic of the talk or the stand ("Which of your customers are already banking somewhere else?"). Up to 12 words.
- The organiser's event mark as supplied artwork, in its version for dark backgrounds.
- The talk in one statement and one supporting line: what the audience will see, not who we are.
- The speaker: a real square headshot, name and role. Optional for a stand-only post.
- Event name, dates, city, stand, talk time, and a booking URL for the caption.

**Build rules**
- Cover: lockup top-left, `title-question` centred at top ≈ 380px, the event mark bottom-centre above the footer row (about 400 × 200px). Arrow at the margin. No motif.
- Talk: eyebrow "The talk", `title-statement` with up to 3 words in `gold-light`, one `body-lead` line.
- Speaker: eyebrow "Speaker", the person row (200px square headshot with 6px corners and a `paper-35` outline, name in Plex Sans SemiBold 40, role in `paper-72`), then one `title-statement` about what they'll show.
- Invite (the last slide): `title-cover` "Meet us at [event]" centred at top 192px; details below at x 300px: calendar, pin and clock icons at 56px with `body` text, 32px apart; then one button (`paper` fill, `navy` text, 4px corners, "Book a slot"). The mark bottom-right; no arrow, no counter.
- Partner artwork is never placed on a white box and never recoloured.

**Don't**
- Don't redraw or recolour the organiser's mark, or use their logo without permission.
- Don't put the URL, a QR code or a hashtag on a slide. The link lives in the caption.
- Don't use urgency ("Last chance", "Only 3 slots left") or exclamation marks on slides.
- Don't use a stock photo or an illustration for the speaker.
