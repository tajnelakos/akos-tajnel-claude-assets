# RegulationBrief

Explains what a rule or supervisory change means for lenders and insurers: the rule as a question, what it says, what it asks, what to prepare, where Seekridge fits, and a close.

**Use it for** regulation, supervisory guidance and audit expectations that touch automated customer decisions: the EU AI Act, model-risk guidance, consumer-outcome rules. The reader is the Head of Credit Risk, Vendor Risk & Compliance or IT Security. 6 slides.

**You provide**
- The rule's name and the relevant part, exactly as published ("EU AI Act · Annex III"), checked against the official text.
- The cover as a question the rule puts to the reader ("Credit scoring is high-risk AI. Can you explain yours?").
- What the rule says, in one sentence a non-lawyer can repeat, with up to 3 words highlighted.
- Three obligations it brings, each a full sentence.
- Three preparation steps, numbered, each starting with a verb.
- Where Seekridge fits, as two or three named capabilities (Evidence Trail, audit log) with one line each.
- A close that points to the full brief, and the line "not legal advice" in some form.

**Build rules**
- Cover: lockup top-left, `ridge-lines` motif top-right, `title-cover` anchored 240px from the bottom and 120px clear of the right edge, plus a `body-lead` line saying how many slides.
- Rule: the rule's reference in `data-label` mono above a `title-statement`, the key words in `gold-light`.
- Obligations: a paper slide, `heading` plus a three-row checklist.
- Preparation: navy, three `navy-deep` panels 32px apart, each with a mono number (01, 02, 03) as its label and a `body` line.
- Fit: a paper slide with two white cards, each with a 64px icon (evidence-trail, policy), a `card-title` and one `card-body` line.
- Close: as every close; the not-legal-advice line goes in `body-lead`.

**Don't**
- Don't paraphrase a rule loosely, add dates or deadlines you haven't checked, or imply Seekridge makes a customer compliant.
- Don't use fear: no "fines of up to…" covers, red, or warning icons.
- Don't give legal advice. Say what to prepare and who to ask.
- Don't name a regulator's enforcement case or a bank that was fined.
