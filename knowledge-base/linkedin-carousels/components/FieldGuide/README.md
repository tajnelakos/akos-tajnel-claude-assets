# FieldGuide

A numbered list: a count-led cover, one slide per check, step or question, a checklist summary on paper, and a close.

**Use it for** practical value a reader saves and forwards: checks before a vendor review, steps to prepare for a backtest, questions for a risk committee. The format invites saving, so every item must stand alone. 5–8 slides (3–5 items).

**You provide**
- The count and the promise for the cover ("3 checks before you trust a customer score") and who it's for ("For heads of model risk.").
- Per item: a headline of up to 7 words (`title-step`) that starts with a verb, and a white card of up to 40 words that says why it matters and what to ask.
- A one-line version of each item for the checklist slide.
- A close that turns the list into an action.

**Build rules**
- Cover: lockup top-left; `figure` (128px) for the count, then `title-cover` for the promise and `body-lead` for the reader, as one block anchored 240px from the bottom. No motif. Arrow at the margin.
- Items: eyebrow "Field guide"; `numeral` (160px, two digits: 01, 02, 03) top-left at 192px; `title-step` at top 400px; a white card 48px below it (6px corners, padding 48px, `card-body` in `ink`). No gold on item slides unless one word in the headline earns it.
- Checklist: a paper slide, `heading` "The short version" (or similar) plus one checklist row per item.
- Close: as every close.

**Don't**
- Don't exceed 5 items. Split into a series instead.
- Don't number with a single digit on item slides, or mix numerals and bullets.
- Don't let a card run past 40 words; cut, don't shrink.
- Don't make an item depend on the one before it ("As above…").
