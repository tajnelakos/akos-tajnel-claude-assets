# Example: a Moment explainer for Head of Retail Banking

A worked run of [`skill.md`](./skill.md). The rendered slides are in [`assets/References/moment-explainer.jpg`](./assets/References/moment-explainer.jpg) and the build is [`components/MomentExplainer/preview.html`](./components/MomentExplainer/preview.html).

## Brief

- **Angle:** the primary message in [`positioning.md`](../positioning.md), *opportunities you can act on — and explain*, shown through one outflow moment. No figure is claimed, so nothing needs a source beyond "illustrative".
- **Reader:** Head of Retail Banking (economic buyer, see [`personas.md`](../personas.md)). They want more opportunities worked faster; their risk colleague wants to know why each customer was chosen. The carousel answers both.
- **Format:** MomentExplainer, 6 slides, EN-GB.

## Choices worth knowing

- **The cover is a question that names a situation**, the one question form the wording rules allow. A rhetorical opener ("Tired of lists nobody can explain?") would fail.
- **The motif carries the cover's gold**, so the cover title has no highlighted words.
- **Slide 2 states the tension without a banned contrast.** "A score tells you who. It rarely tells you why." makes two plain statements. An earlier draft of slide 5, "Call with a reason, not a script.", was rewritten because it read as a slogan, and the carousel already spends its one contrast on slide 2.
- **The proof is on paper**, the one ground change in the carousel, so the reader sees where the evidence starts.
- **Weights add up to 1.00**, and the source line says they're illustrative.
- **The close offers a backtest on the reader's own data**, the precise CTA the golden rules in [`../design-system/writing.md`](../design-system/writing.md) use instead of "Learn more".
