The Seekridge logo, redrawn for the carousel canvas: the wordmark is outlined (paths, not live text) and the grounds are transparent, so each file sits directly on a flat navy or paper slide. `pw-lockup-paper.svg` (paper strokes, gold-light point) goes on navy; `pw-lockup-navy.svg` (navy strokes, gold point) on paper. The lockup is 320 × 80px on covers and closes; the mark is 56 × 56px bottom-right on every other slide. The source files with live text, the palette sheet and the misuse sheet stay in `knowledge-base/branding-guideline/`.
