Line icons from the design system (1.75px stroke on a 24px grid, square caps, no fills), in two colourways: `-paper` for navy grounds and `-navy` for paper grounds and white cards. Use them at 48–64px on the canvas. The three data layers always use `transaction` (transaction data), `customer` (customer data) and `market` (market data). `calendar`, `pin` and `clock` are drawn for event details. Never fill, recolour or mix in another icon set.
