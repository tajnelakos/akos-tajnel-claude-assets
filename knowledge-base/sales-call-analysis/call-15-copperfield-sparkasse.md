**Anna:** Owen, thanks for booking time. I saw you came in through a referral rather than one of our usual channels. Who pointed you our way?

**Owen:** Exactly our situation. We're a small team, 15 advisors, mostly experienced people, not a lot of hand-holding needed. I don't want a long sales process. If this does what my friend described, I'd rather move quickly.

**Anna:** Given your team's already tool-comfortable and experienced, onboarding should be straightforward. Advisors are typically productive from their first real call, without extensive training.

**Owen:** Good. Pricing?

**Anna:** It's priced by the number of customers analysed. At around 82,000 you'd be in [a specific tier]. I can confirm the exact number in writing today rather than have you wait.

**Owen:** That's reasonable based on what I expected going in. Is there a trial option, or does this need a full commitment upfront?

**Anna:** We can run a short pilot with a handful of your advisors first if you'd prefer to validate before rolling out to everyone, though given what you've described, a full rollout might also be reasonable if you're confident based on your colleague's experience.

**Owen:** Given how specific and consistent what I heard was, I think we just move forward with the full team. I don't need to redo the diligence my friend already did.

**Anna:** That's your call to make, and I respect the confidence. I'll send the pricing confirmation and a simple implementation plan today so we can move at the pace you're looking for.

**Anna:** I'll have everything to you by end of day.
