# Call Transcript — Harborlight Credit Union

**Date:** August 2026 · **Duration:** ~13 minutes · **Call type:** First discovery call
**Rep:** Marcus Webb (Seekridge) · **Prospect:** Dana Whitfield, Head of Advisory Network, Harborlight Credit Union (credit union, DACH, ~29,000 members, ~60 advisors)

---

**Marcus:** Dana, thanks for the time. What made you take this call?

**Dana:** Curiosity more than urgency, I'll be honest. We've used Nudgely in our app for about two years, and our advisors get a weekly export from it. It's not perfect, but it's embedded: advisors know it, it's in their routine.

**Marcus:** Given that, what's the actual motivation to even take a call like this?

**Dana:** A newer advisor who came from another bank mentioned your platform and said the moments were noticeably better, with the reason behind each one. That planted a seed, but I want to be upfront: switching 60 advisors off an embedded routine is a real undertaking, not something I'd do for a marginal improvement.

**Marcus:** That's a fair bar to set, and I'd rather be direct about what would and wouldn't justify that disruption. Can I ask what specifically that advisor said was better?

**Dana:** Mostly that it flagged members who were moving money to other banks, with the payments behind it, so an advisor could explain why they were calling.

**Marcus:** That tracks with how we're built: the goal is giving an advisor something they can actually walk a member through, not just a score. But I want to be honest with you. Is "better moments with evidence" actually a top-3 problem for your team today, or a nice-to-have that one advisor happened to notice?

**Dana:** Fair question. Honestly, no. Adoption and consistency across 60 advisors is my actual top problem, not the depth of any single tool's output. Half my team barely opens the weekly export as it is.

**Marcus:** Then I don't think an "our moments are better" pitch is the right conversation for you right now, and I'd rather tell you that than manufacture urgency that isn't real. If the real problem is inconsistent adoption of what you already have, switching platforms doesn't obviously fix that. It might just reset the adoption curve to zero and create a worse problem for a while.

**Dana:** That's refreshingly candid for a sales call, I'll say that.

**Marcus:** I'd rather have a shorter, honest conversation than a longer one that leads nowhere. Here's what I'd suggest instead: keep our details on file. If Nudgely's limitations ever become an actual blocker — not "one advisor noticed something," but a real operational problem — that's the moment this conversation is worth having properly, likely alongside a broader adoption push whichever platform you land on.

**Dana:** That's a reasonable way to leave it. I'll keep you in mind if something changes structurally, not just anecdotally.

**Marcus:** Sounds good. No follow-up pressure from my side. I'm glad you had a clear-eyed read on your own priority.
