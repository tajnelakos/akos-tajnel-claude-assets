New findings worth escalating

Things that surfaced from real calls and aren't yet reflected in competitive-landscape.md or elsewhere — this is the actual point of running this rollup, not just counting mentions
Compliance
On-prem deployment gap confirmed as a live, hard deal-blocker
Previously a documented but somewhat abstract known gap. Now confirmed as an actual, board-level, non-negotiable requirement at a real Tier 2 account, tied to a specific data-residency regulation. Worth prioritizing a real roadmap conversation rather than continuing to treat it as deprioritized.
Source: call-10-solvane-bank-group.md
Compliance
New named regulatory certification requirement, not previously tracked
A specific national certification for automated decision models came up as a requirement, with "in progress, credible timeline" acceptable to the prospect's board — not a hard no if we can commit to a roadmap. Needs a real answer from compliance regardless of this deal's outcome, since it may recur in the same market.
Source: call-11-crestview-capital-bank.md
Data
Unconfirmed data coverage gap in smaller regional markets
A prospect flagged that ~15% of their customers sit in two smaller regional markets where our merchant-categorisation coverage may be thinner than the long-tenured incumbent's. Not yet confirmed either way — a real answer from the data team is a hard blocker for this specific deal and worth checking proactively before it recurs.
Source: call-05-baltic-savings-bank.md
Product
Mobile experience isn't yet strong enough for true field use
Honestly disclosed rather than oversold in a credit-union call — the desktop/tablet experience covers weekly call planning well, but not a banker checking a member on a phone just before a visit. Worth tracking whether this recurs before treating it as a roadmap priority.
Source: call-04-riverton-credit-union.md
Customer Success
No confirmed simplified onboarding path for less tech-comfortable users
A credit-union buyer's real blocker wasn't price — it was uncertainty about adoption across a team with mixed tech comfort. Worth confirming whether a lighter onboarding track exists, since this may be suppressing Tier 3 deal size or close rate more broadly than this one account.
Source: call-08-fenwick-oak-credit-union.md
Battlecard
NexaScore's moments reportedly weaker for sole traders and small-business owners than salaried customers
More granular than anything currently in the NexaScore battlecard, which treats the post-GA gap as uniform. If this holds up across more calls, it's a sharper, more specific wedge than the current "unproven at scale" framing — worth feeding into battlecard/nexascore.html directly.
Source: call-09-ironbridge-bank.md

Recurring pattern: vendor stability at larger accounts

Two separate Tier 1/Tier 2 accounts independently raised company size / vendor stability as the single remaining objection after product diligence was otherwise complete — not previously documented as a recurring pattern anywhere in the knowledge base. Both calls converged on the same effective response: offer concrete data-portability terms and a real similar-sized-customer count, rather than a general reassurance. Worth writing this up as a standing objection-handling entry rather than re-deriving it per deal.
Sources: call-03-court-street-bank.md, call-06-alpine-credit-union.md
