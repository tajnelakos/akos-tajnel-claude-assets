# Call Transcript — Riverton Savings Bank

**Date:** July 2026 · **Duration:** ~16 minutes · **Call type:** First discovery call
**Rep:** Marcus Webb (Seekridge) · **Prospect:** Tomas Reyes, Head of Advisory Network, Riverton Savings Bank (small savings bank, DACH, ~74,000 customers, ~40 advisors)

---

**Marcus:** Tomas, thanks for hopping on. I saw you downloaded the advisor productivity piece. What prompted that?

**Tomas:** We're looking for a way to show our advisors which customers to call. Right now they work from campaign lists our CRM builds once a quarter, or wait for me to guess who might be ready, which doesn't scale past a certain size. And when they do get the appointment, they're often guessing at why the customer would want to talk.

**Marcus:** Makes sense. Walk me through what a customer conversation looks like today without a tool.

**Tomas:** Two problems. First, we find out a customer moved their mortgage or their salary to another bank after it's happened. The payments were going through our books the whole time. Second, when an advisor does get a meeting, they open with a product, not with a reason, and the customer can tell. We lose those conversations to banks that call with something specific.

**Marcus:** Got it. So the tool's job is to get your advisors to the right customers first, and then give them the reason for the call, not just produce a report afterward.

**Tomas:** Right. Our digital team actually piloted Nudgely in the app last year.

**Marcus:** What was their read on it?

**Tomas:** Mixed. Customers opened the insights, a few times. But nothing ever reached an advisor, so it didn't change a single conversation, and it quietly stopped. Which is my actual worry with any tool: adoption by the advisors, not features.

**Marcus:** That's the right worry to have, honestly. A lot of advisor tools die in month two, not because the feature set was wrong but because they added friction instead of removing it. Can I ask what made your last advisor tool fail?

**Tomas:** The CRM campaign module? It wasn't built for how our advisors work. A third of them see customers outside the branch, at home or at the farm, on a tablet or a phone. They'd have to get back to a laptop to see why a customer was on the list.

**Marcus:** That's a real gap, and I want to be straight with you rather than oversell. Our mobile view today shows the moment and its evidence, but it's not yet as polished as I'd want for an advisor sitting at a customer's kitchen table. It's on our roadmap, but I'm not going to claim it's solved if it isn't.

**Tomas:** I appreciate you saying that directly instead of dancing around it. How far off is it?

**Marcus:** I don't have an exact date I can commit to on this call. I'd rather follow up with a real answer from our product team than guess. What I can say is the desktop and tablet experience is strong today, and for advisors preparing before a meeting rather than live in it, it already solves the core problem you described.

**Tomas:** That's actually most of our use case, if I'm honest. The "at the kitchen table" scenario is maybe 20% of it. The bigger issue is knowing who to call this week, and having the reason ready before the meeting.

**Marcus:** Then that's worth testing directly rather than theorizing about it. Would a small pilot with maybe five advisors make sense, the ones already comfortable with tools, not your most skeptical group, before rolling out to all 40?

**Tomas:** That's smart, actually. Doing it broadly and having it fail on adoption would set us back further than testing it small first.

**Marcus:** I'll follow up on the mobile roadmap separately so you have a real answer, not a placeholder. For the pilot, do you want to pick the five advisors, or should we suggest a mix of tenure and comfort levels?

**Tomas:** I'll pick them. I know who'd give it a fair shot and who'd look for reasons to go back to old habits.

**Marcus:** Sounds good. I'll send pilot details and follow up on mobile by end of week.

**Tomas:** Appreciate it. Let's see how the five-advisor group does before we talk numbers for the whole network.
