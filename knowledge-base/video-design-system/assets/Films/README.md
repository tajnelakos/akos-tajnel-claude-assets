# Films

The reference films, one per format plus a short segment cut, built in code from this system and rendered with [`../../films/render-film.py`](../../films/render-film.py). Their HTML sources are in [`../../films/`](../../films). 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264, captions burnt in, each with an `.srt` sidecar. They ship **silent**, apart from the segment film's draft voice: no voice or music is cleared yet (film book, section 13). The segment film's voice-over script is ready for ElevenLabs.

| File | Format | Length | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `segment-mortgage-lending-16x9.mp4` | Segment story (short cut) | 56.2 s | Retail and mortgage banking, for both buyers (Head of Mortgage Lending, Head of Retail Banking), value → benefit → feature: customers' plans rising from the book as a moving dot map, your CRM and transaction data read alongside live market data, then four moments, each with its evidence and the offer it opens (growing family → mortgage for a bigger home; buying a car → car loan before the dealer's finance; renovating → green loan; new home detected with no cover → home insurance), the week's moments settling into a ranked CRM queue, and a closing call to action (*Your next deals are hiding in your client base.* → *Run a test on your own book. See your next deals.*). **Has a draft voice-over** (offline Kokoro voice, a placeholder until the ElevenLabs read). Voice-over script: [`segment-mortgage-lending-16x9-voiceover.md`](./segment-mortgage-lending-16x9-voiceover.md) |
| `story-fixed-rate-dach-16x9.mp4` | Moment story | 48 s | Head of Mortgage Lending, DACH lender: a fixed rate ending, the refinance-risk moment ranked third, its evidence trail, the CRM hand-off, the backtest figure, the end card |
| `walkthrough-evidence-trail-16x9.mp4` | Walkthrough | 30 s | The Evidence Trail feature in four steps: open, weights, export for model risk, audit log; two-line lockup |
| `statement-evidence-16x9.mp4` | Statement | 26.5 s | *Most moments can't explain themselves. Ours show their evidence.* The segment triptych, the figure, the end card |

All figures are labelled illustrative on screen; the lender, customer IDs and data are fictional.
