# Films

The reference films, one per format plus a short segment cut, built in code from this system and rendered with [`../../films/render-film.py`](../../films/render-film.py). Their HTML sources are in [`../../films/`](../../films). 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264, captions burnt in, each with an `.srt` sidecar. They ship **silent** with captions: no voice or music is cleared yet (film book, section 13). The segment film's voice-over script is ready for ElevenLabs.

| File | Format | Length | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `segment-retail-lending-16x9.mp4` | Segment story (short cut) | 50.2 s | Retail lending, value → benefit → feature: customers' plans rising as a moving dot map, your transactions, savings and AI-agent conversations read alongside market data, then three moments each with its evidence and the offer it opens (mortgage-ready → mortgage offer; family growing → a bigger home, ranked as the one next step; car purchase goal → car loan before the dealer's finance), the week's moments settling into a ranked CRM queue, and a closing call to action that answers the opening (*Your next loans are in your data.* → *Run a test on your own data. See your next loans.*). **Ships silent with captions** for now: the earlier draft voice no longer matches the new lines and needs re-recording (ElevenLabs, or the offline Kokoro voice). Voice-over script: [`segment-retail-lending-16x9-voiceover.md`](./segment-retail-lending-16x9-voiceover.md) |
| `story-outflow-dach-16x9.mp4` | Moment story | 48 s | Head of Deposits & Savings, DACH savings bank: a customer starting to send EUR 400 a month to another bank, the outflow moment ranked third, its evidence trail, the CRM hand-off, the backtest figure, the end card |
| `walkthrough-evidence-trail-16x9.mp4` | Walkthrough | 30 s | The Evidence Trail feature in four steps: open, weights, export for model risk, audit log; two-line lockup |
| `statement-evidence-16x9.mp4` | Statement | 26.5 s | *Most moments can't explain themselves. Ours show their evidence.* The segment triptych, the figure, the end card |

All figures are labelled illustrative on screen; the bank, customer IDs and data are fictional.
