# Films

The reference films, one per format plus a short segment cut, built in code from this system and rendered with [`../../films/render-film.py`](../../films/render-film.py). Their HTML sources are in [`../../films/`](../../films). 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264, captions burnt in, each with an `.srt` sidecar. They ship **silent** with captions: no voice or music is cleared yet (film book, section 13). The segment film's voice-over script is ready for ElevenLabs.

| File | Format | Length | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `segment-retail-lending-16x9.mp4` | Segment story (short cut) | 64.3 s | Retail banking, value → benefit → feature: customers' plans rising as a moving dot map, transactions, market data, AI conversations, mortgage and client data combined to find the right moment, then four moments each with its evidence and the offer it opens (growing family → a bigger-home mortgage; buying a car → car loan before the dealer's finance; renovation planned (AI chats about energy renovation value, savings set aside) → green loan; money starting to leave → a savings offer before the balance falls), the week's moments settling into a ranked CRM queue, and a brand close: the large centred logo, the slogan *Be first to their next plan.*. Carries a **draft voice** (offline Kokoro, voice *bf_emma*, −16 LUFS) until the ElevenLabs recording replaces it. Voice-over script: [`segment-retail-lending-16x9-voiceover.md`](./segment-retail-lending-16x9-voiceover.md) |
| `story-outflow-dach-16x9.mp4` | Moment story | 48 s | Head of Deposits & Savings, DACH savings bank: a customer starting to send EUR 400 a month to another bank, the outflow moment ranked third, its evidence trail, the CRM hand-off, the backtest figure, the end card |
| `walkthrough-evidence-trail-16x9.mp4` | Walkthrough | 30 s | The Evidence Trail feature in four steps: open, every reason in plain words, export for model risk, audit log; two-line lockup |
| `statement-evidence-16x9.mp4` | Statement | 26.5 s | *Most moments can't explain themselves. Ours show their evidence.* The segment triptych, the figure, the end card |

All figures are labelled illustrative on screen; the bank, customer names and IDs, and data are fictional.
