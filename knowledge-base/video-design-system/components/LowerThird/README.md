# LowerThird

Names who or what is on screen when the picture can't: a role, a team, a data source. Never a person's name: Seekridge films show roles.

**Anatomy.** Navy band at 92% (`caption-band`), 2px gold rule on top, Plex Sans 600 40px for the label, Plex Mono 22px for the qualifier. Left-aligned at the margin, its bottom edge 120px above the caption band, so both can be on screen together.

**Motion.** Fades in with the beat's first change, holds at least 3 s, fades out with the beat. No slide, no wipe.

**Copy.** Label: *Retention team, Hamburg branch*, *Transaction data*, *Head of Credit Risk view*. Qualifier: *Demo · Alpenkreis Bank*, *Updated 2026-11-24*.

**Provide.** The label and the qualifier.

**Avoid.** Real names, job titles of real people, logos in the band, more than one LowerThird per beat. Its gold rule counts as the frame's gold, so no highlight in the headline while it's on.
