# EndCard

How every Moment story and Statement closes. Walkthroughs close on the two-line lockup variant instead.

**Anatomy**
- Navy ground, nothing else on it: no motif, no field, no chapter rail.
- Reversed lockup, centred, 420px wide.
- The line in `end-line` (Archivo 700, 64px): the brand line *Opportunities you can act on — and explain.* unless the brief approves another.
- One action in Plex Sans 500, 32px, `ink-muted`, with the arrow icon: *Run a backtest on your own book*. One action only, never a list of links.

**Variant: Walkthrough lockup.** Paper ground, two centred lines in `headline` (Archivo 700, 72px), line 1 ink, line 2 with the gold underline bar; the mark 48px bottom-right; no lockup. *Every moment. / Its reasons, on screen.*

**Variant: Closing claim** (segment films). Navy, a RidgeField band along the bottom with no gold point, the reversed lockup small at the top (320px), a two-line claim in `title` that answers the film's opening hook with the payoff in gold-light (*Now you know who. / And why.*), and the action as a Paper button with navy text (10px corners). The brand line is said in the voice-over and shown in the caption, not set on the card. Reference: the end of `segment-mortgage-lending-16x9`.

**Motion.** Lockup fades in (0.2 s), the line rises (0.5 s), the action fades in (1.2 s). Then everything holds still for `end-hold` (3 s). No voice-over and no caption over the last 2 s.

**Provide.** The action (a verb and an object the viewer can do this week).

**Avoid.** URLs in place of an action, QR codes, social icons, a second line of copy, music swelling into the end.
