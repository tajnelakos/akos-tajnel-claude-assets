# ParcelField

The brand's one graphic idea, at the scale of a whole customer base: land divided by straight lines, each lot a customer (or a group of them), the week's moments marked, the one the film follows in gold.

**Anatomy**
- An SVG field of lots, generated by straight subdivision (longest lines first, no diagonals, no curves).
- Lines: `survey-line` at `survey-line-opacity` (Navy at 18% on Paper, Paper at 35% on Navy), 2px, square caps.
- Marked lots: filled navy at 90% on Paper (Paper at 75% on Navy), inset 6px from their lines.
- One gold lot: the customer the film follows, or the logomark's top-right lot on a TitleCard.

**Variants.** Full field (scale beat), right-edge field (TitleCard), bottom band (StatementStage), small field inside a Walkthrough frame.

**Motion.** The outline draws, then divisions longest first (`draw`, staggered); marked lots fill one after another (60ms apart); the gold lot fills last (`lot-fill`). A slow straight push-in (`drift`) is allowed on the scale beat.

**Copy.** The headline gives the count: *212 customers to call this week. Not 40,000.* The field is never labelled lot by lot.

**Provide.** Size, seed, how many lots to mark, where the gold lot goes.

**Avoid.** A map of a real place, house icons, people, dots instead of lots, heat-map colour, more than one gold lot.
