# SegmentTriptych

Proof of breadth for Statements and segment overviews: the same moment-plus-evidence grammar for a bank, an insurer and a brokerage, side by side.

**Anatomy.** Three equal ProductPanels across columns 1–12 with 24px gutters. Each: mono `SEGMENT · DEMO` label, the moment name in Plex Sans 600 34px, two evidence lines with weights and bars (navy only: no gold here, the gold is in the headline).

**Segment pairs** (from `../knowledge-base.md`): Bank → Refinance risk · Insurer → Underinsured home · Brokerage → Likely to sell.

**Motion.** Panels trace in left to right 350ms apart; evidence lines and bars follow inside each. Hold 2 s.

**Provide.** Three moments and their top two evidence lines.

**Avoid.** Industry icons or photos, different layouts per panel, a fourth panel, colours per segment.
