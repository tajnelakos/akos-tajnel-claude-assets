# SegmentTriptych

Proof of breadth for Statements and segment overviews: the same moment-plus-evidence grammar for a main-bank, an outflow and a growth moment, side by side.

**Anatomy.** Three equal ProductPanels across columns 1–12 with 24px gutters. Each: mono `FAMILY · DEMO` label, the moment name in Plex Sans 600 34px, two evidence lines with weights and bars (navy only: no gold here, the gold is in the headline).

**Family pairs** (from `../knowledge-base.md`): Main bank → Salary landing elsewhere · Outflow → Money starting to leave · Growth → Mortgage-ready.

**Motion.** Panels trace in left to right 350ms apart; evidence lines and bars follow inside each. Hold 2 s.

**Provide.** Three moments and their top two evidence lines.

**Avoid.** Industry icons or photos, different layouts per panel, a fourth panel, colours per family.
