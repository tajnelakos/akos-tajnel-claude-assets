# StatementStage

The ground of a Statement film: Navy throughout, a band of parcel lines along the bottom edge, one claim in `title` type. It is the Parcelwise answer to a launch stage: no devices, no glow, the idea carried by type and one drawing.

**Anatomy**
- Navy ground, full frame.
- Parcel band: the field cut to a 300px strip along the bottom, no gold lot (the gold sits in the headline).
- Mono eyebrow at the margin, then the claim in `title`, left-aligned, max two lines, up to three words in gold-light.
- No logo until the EndCard.

**Motion.** The band draws in first (1.5 s, no words), then the eyebrow, then the claim line by line. The next statement replaces the claim with a fade-out and new lines rising; the band stays.

**Provide.** The claim (one sentence, full stop), the turn (the next beat), the eyebrow.

**Use.** Statement films; the Proof beat of a Moment story can borrow the stage for its FigureCard.

**Avoid.** Hype in the claim (*revolutionary*, *game-changing*), a product panel in the first beat, a band with a gold lot and a gold headline in the same frame.
