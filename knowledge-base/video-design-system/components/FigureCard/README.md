# FigureCard

The Proof beat: one number, what it measures, where it came from.

**Anatomy**
- Navy ground (Paper allowed in Walkthroughs).
- The figure in `figure` (Archivo 800, 240px), tabular, left at the margin.
- The measure under it in Plex Sans 500 44px, max 26 characters a line: *of flagged customers refinanced elsewhere within six months*.
- The source in mono 22px under a 48px rule: *Backtest, demo lender's 2025 book (n = 1,940). Illustrative.* Mandatory.

**Motion.** The figure counts up from zero over 800ms and lands on the exact value (`count`); the measure rises 300ms after; the source fades in last. Hold at least 3 s.

**Numbers.** One figure per card. Real figures need an approved case and its source; anything else says *Illustrative*. Market formats: *68%* (UK), *68 %* (DE, Nordics), currency code first (*EUR 2.4m*).

**Provide.** The value, the measure, the source and its period.

**Avoid.** Two figures, a figure without a source, rounding up for drama (*~70%*), gold on the figure (the figure is the point already).
