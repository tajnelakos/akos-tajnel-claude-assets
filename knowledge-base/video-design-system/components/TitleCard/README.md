# TitleCard

How Moment stories open: the customer's situation as a one-line hook on the Navy ground, with the dot map moving in from the right edge, a few peaks rising and its one gold point landing.

**Anatomy**
- Lockup, reversed, top-left inside the 96px margin (`lockup-w` 360px).
- Ridge field (see RidgeField) anchored to the right edge, 600px wide (the last two columns and the margin), full height. Dots in Paper, brightest at the peaks. The hook never runs into it.
- Optional mono eyebrow naming the segment and persona: `DEPOSITS & SAVINGS · DACH`.
- The hook as `title` (Archivo 800, 120px), bottom-left, max two lines, max 1180px wide so it never runs into the field's gold point.

**Motion** (0–5 s)
1. 0.2 s: the field's outer line draws, then its divisions, longest first (`draw`, `draw-stagger`).
2. ~2.1 s: the gold point fills from its bottom edge (`lot-fill`): the signature move, with the one soft tick if there is sound.
3. 0.6 s: the lockup fades in; 1.0 s: the eyebrow; 1.3 s: the hook rises line by line (`line-in`).
4. Hold to 5 s. No voice-over for the first 1.5 s.

**Provide.** The hook (the customer's situation, 4–8 words, full stop), the eyebrow, the field angle if you want a different plan.

**Rules**
- The hook names a situation, never the product: *They just started sending money elsewhere.* Not *Meet Seekridge Moments.*
- Up to three words in gold-light are allowed, but the gold point already is the frame's gold: prefer no highlight.
- Walkthroughs and Statements don't use a TitleCard.

**Avoid.** A second motif, a photo, a gradient behind the title, the title over the field's lines.
