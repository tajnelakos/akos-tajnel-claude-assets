# TitleCard

How Moment stories open: the customer's situation as a one-line hook on the Navy ground, with the parcel field drawing in from the right edge and its one lot filling gold.

**Anatomy**
- Lockup, reversed, top-left inside the 96px margin (`lockup-w` 360px).
- Parcel field (see ParcelField) anchored to the right edge, 600px wide (the last two columns and the margin), full height. Lines at 35% Paper. The hook never runs into it.
- Optional mono eyebrow naming the segment and persona: `MORTGAGE LENDING · DACH`.
- The hook as `title` (Archivo 800, 120px), bottom-left, max two lines, max 1180px wide so it never runs into the field's gold lot.

**Motion** (0–5 s)
1. 0.2 s: the field's outer line draws, then its divisions, longest first (`draw`, `draw-stagger`).
2. ~2.1 s: the gold lot fills from its bottom edge (`lot-fill`): the signature move, with the one soft tick if there is sound.
3. 0.6 s: the lockup fades in; 1.0 s: the eyebrow; 1.3 s: the hook rises line by line (`line-in`).
4. Hold to 5 s. No voice-over for the first 1.5 s.

**Provide.** The hook (the customer's situation, 4–8 words, full stop), the eyebrow, the field angle if you want a different plan.

**Rules**
- The hook names a situation, never the product: *Their fixed rate ends in four months.* Not *Meet Parcelwise Moments.*
- Up to three words in gold-light are allowed, but the gold lot already is the frame's gold: prefer no highlight.
- Walkthroughs and Statements don't use a TitleCard.

**Avoid.** A second motif, a photo, a gradient behind the title, the title over the field's lines.
