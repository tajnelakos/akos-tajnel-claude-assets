# Buyer interviews — trailing 12 months (sample)

Eight interview summaries, run by product marketing two to four weeks after each decision, using the [interview guide](./interview-guide.md). Each one joins to its row in the [CRM export](./win-loss-crm-export.csv) by Opportunity ID. Names and quotes are fictional; Seekridge is a fictional company.

Eight interviews is below the 15–20 a quarter the [pipeline](./interview-guide.md#the-pipeline) aims for, so read the pattern as a strong hint, not a measured rate.

Rep and buyer fully agreed in 2 of 8 interviews, partly in 2, and differed in 4. Three of the four lost deals where they differed were logged as price or data coverage; the buyer named something else.

## Lost deals

### OPP-1003 · Court Street Bank · lost to NexaScore · €154,764

- **Rep logged:** Price.
- **Buyer said:** NexaScore was a little cheaper, but that wasn't it. They asked both vendors for a bank of their size already live. NexaScore named two; we named none.
- **Competitor view:** NexaScore passed the same live-demo test we set for them, so the demo stopped being a difference.
- **Quote:** "The price gap was small. The reference gap wasn't."
- **Goes to:** Positioning (reference programme for Tier 1), Sales training (the "who else our size uses this" question).

### OPP-1020 · Ravensworth Bank Group · lost to NexaScore · €126,372

- **Rep logged:** Data coverage gap.
- **Buyer said:** Coverage was fine for their markets. Procurement needed a specific regulatory certification on the vendor list, and we couldn't show it.
- **Competitor view:** NexaScore had it, and put it on page one of their proposal.
- **Quote:** "It was a box on the procurement form. You couldn't tick it."
- **Goes to:** Roadmap (certification, with this deal's ACV attached), Battlecards (NexaScore leads with it).

### OPP-1005 · Baltic Savings Bank · lost to Conturo · €49,193

- **Rep logged:** Data coverage gap.
- **Buyer said:** They asked about coverage in two regional markets. The answer took three weeks. By then the Conturo renewal date had come and they renewed for two years.
- **Competitor view:** Conturo didn't win on product; it won on being there and renewing on time.
- **Quote:** "If we'd had the answer in a week, we'd have run a pilot."
- **Goes to:** Sales training (escalating blocker questions fast), Battlecards (find the incumbent's renewal date early).

### OPP-1012 · Harborlight Credit Union · lost to Pocketlens · €25,236

- **Rep logged:** Adoption risk / incumbent inertia.
- **Buyer said:** The rep read it right: better reasons to call members wasn't a top-three problem this year. They added one thing the rep didn't hear. Per-seat pricing across 60 bankers made it look like a big project, when they wanted to start with ten.
- **Competitor view:** Pocketlens is already in the member app, so it felt like no extra work.
- **Quote:** "Let us start small and we'd have talked again."
- **Goes to:** Roadmap/pricing team (a small-start option for credit unions; a finding, not a pricing decision), Positioning (sit next to Pocketlens rather than against it).

### OPP-1010 · Solvane Bank Group · lost to Conturo · €192,775

- **Rep logged:** No on-prem deployment option.
- **Buyer said:** The same. Their regulator wouldn't accept the cloud set-up for this data.
- **Competitor view:** Conturo runs on-prem; that was the whole decision.
- **Quote:** "We liked the product. We couldn't host it."
- **Goes to:** Roadmap (on-prem, with this deal's ACV attached).

### OPP-1007 · Delta Direct Bank · no decision · €90,672

- **Rep logged:** No decision – internal bandwidth.
- **Buyer said:** The same. A core banking migration took every team for the year. They want to talk again after it ends.
- **Competitor view:** None chosen; Ledgerline was also parked.
- **Quote:** "It wasn't a no. It was a not this year."
- **Goes to:** Nurture list with a date, not the loss pile.

## Won deals

### OPP-1001 · Nordkredit · won against Conturo · €56,924

- **Rep logged:** Explainability / evidence trail.
- **Buyer said:** The evidence trail mattered, but the moment of decision was the backtest on their own customers. Seeing who would have been flagged last year, and what happened next, settled it.
- **What almost went the other way:** Switching cost from Conturo.
- **Quote:** "Your slides said it works. Our own data showed it."
- **Goes to:** Positioning (lead with a backtest on the bank's own data), Battlecards vs Conturo.

### OPP-1002 · Meridian Savings · won against Ledgerline · €61,496

- **Rep logged:** Explainability / evidence trail.
- **Buyer said:** Partly that. What they meant was that the review pack let their Head of Model Risk sign off in one meeting. Fast replies from the rep also helped.
- **What almost went the other way:** Ledgerline's lower price.
- **Quote:** "Model risk said yes in one meeting. That never happens."
- **Goes to:** Positioning ("approved once" message), Sales training (bring the review pack early).

## What the interviews changed

1. **Battlecards.** NexaScore leads with its references and its certification, not only its price. Conturo wins on renewal timing. Both go on the cards.
2. **Roadmap.** Two gaps came with real lost ACV: the regulatory certification (€126k) and on-prem (€193k). Product gets both with the deal IDs.
3. **Sales training.** Two scenarios to coach: answering "who else our size uses this?", and getting a blocking question answered within a week.
4. **Positioning.** Buyers who chose us talk about a backtest on their own data and a quick model-risk sign-off, not "explainability". The messaging should say what they say.
