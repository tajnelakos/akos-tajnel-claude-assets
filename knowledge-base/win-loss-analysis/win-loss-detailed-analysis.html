Key findings

  • 1 of 8NexaScore is beating us head to head

    Seven of eight deals against NexaScore were lost, and their quotes ran about 8% below ours.

  • €88k vs €72kWe lose the bigger deals

    Average lost deal against average won deal. Tier 2 wins only 33%, against 53% in Tier 3.

  • 30h vs 37hFaster first replies win

    First response time, won against lost. Demo scores were the same either way (3.8).

  • 28%Many losses aren’t about product

    The largest share of losses came down to process, relationship or timing.
