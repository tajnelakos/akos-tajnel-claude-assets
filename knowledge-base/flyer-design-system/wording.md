# Writing for Seekridge flyers

The voice rules are in [`../branding-guideline/skill.md`](../branding-guideline/skill.md) and the mechanics in [`../design-system/writing.md`](../design-system/writing.md). This file covers what is different on paper: less room, no link to click, and a reader who decides in a few seconds whether to keep the piece.

## The shape of every piece

| Slot | Length | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow | 2–5 words, names the reader | For mortgage lenders |
| Claim (trifold front) | Up to 12 words, one idea per line | Know which customers to call this month. And why. |
| Statement (postcard front) | Up to 8 words | The moment you can explain. |
| Supporting line | One sentence, up to 25 words | Seekridge shows which of your existing customers to talk to next, and why. |
| Problem | Two short paragraphs in the reader's world | Fixed rates end, salaries start landing at another bank and a local lender cuts its two-year rate… |
| Pillars | Title as the claim, body as its proof | Advisors act on it · Every moment shows the data points behind it… |
| Benefits | Up to 8 words each, written as outcomes | Approved once, not list by list |
| Steps | One verb, one sentence | Connect · Your CRM and loan book, through one secure export or API. |
| Figure | Number, what it measures, source | 68% · of flagged customers refinanced within 6 months · Backtest on a lender's 2025 book · illustrative |
| Action | What happens after the scan | Scan to book a backtest |

## Claims

- State the outcome for the reader, in their words: "Know which customers to call this month." Not "AI-driven customer intelligence".
- Gold goes on the words that carry the turn, up to three: "And why.", "explain."
- Questions are fine as a front if the reader can't yet answer them: "Which of your customers will refinance elsewhere this year?"
- Never claim speed or volume alone. If a claim is about more leads, it's the wrong claim.

## Proof

- One moment with its evidence beats three adjectives. Every piece that mentions moments shows an `EvidenceStrip`.
- Up to three figures a piece. Each one has a source line in mono: what the data was, whose it was, when, and "illustrative" if it is.
- Backtests are on the client's own book. Say so: "Backtest on a lender's 2025 book".
- Customers appear by ID. Policies by policy number. Never a real name, address or bank.

## Actions

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Scan to book a backtest | Learn more |
| Scan to see a moment on your own data | Discover the future of banking |
| Book 30 minutes with our team at stand B14 | Contact us today! |

One action a piece. The QR, the printed URL and the action say the same thing.

## Words to avoid

The brand's prohibited terms apply in full: `seamless`, `revolutionary`, `cutting-edge`, `game-changing`, `next-generation`, `unlock`, `robust` (unqualified), `powered by advanced AI`, `disrupt`. On flyers also avoid:

- **"Insights"** for what the product shows. Say "moments".
- **"Leads"** for the client's customers. Say "customers" or "your customer base".
- **"Smart", "intelligent", "AI-powered"** as adjectives. Say what the system checks.
- **Urgency** ("Don't miss out", "Limited time"). This audience reads it as pressure.

## Market formats

| | UK English | German (DE · AT) | Swiss German | French | Nordics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Percent | 68% | 68 % | 68 % | 68 % | 68 % |
| Thousands | 2,140 | 2.140 | 2’140 | 2 140 | 2 140 |
| Currency | EUR 412,500 | EUR 412.500 | CHF 412’500 | EUR 412 500 | SEK 412 500 |
| Quotes | “…” | „…“ | «…» | « … » | ”…” |
| Address | you | Sie | Sie | vous | du |

German runs about 30% longer than English. Write the German claim first when a piece ships in both, and hyphenate long compounds by hand with a soft hyphen (`&shy;`).

## Small print

- Every piece carries "Seekridge is a fictional company. All figures are illustrative." while Seekridge is a portfolio brand.
- Dates in prose: 24 November 2026. In data rows: 2026-11-24.
