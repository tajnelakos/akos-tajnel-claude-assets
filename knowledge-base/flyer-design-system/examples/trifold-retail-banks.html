The problem

Your next deposit outflow is already in your transactions

A salary goes up, a monthly transfer to another bank begins, a digital bank pays a point more on savings. The customer notices. Too often, the bank notices after the money has gone.

Lists pulled by balance threshold miss most of these moments, and none of them can say why one customer matters more than another.

“A lead you can’t explain is a liability, not an opportunity.”
