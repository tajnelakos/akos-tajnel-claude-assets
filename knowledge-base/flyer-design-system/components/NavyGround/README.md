# NavyGround

Flat Seekridge Navy is the brand's front-of-piece surface: every trifold front and back, every postcard front and recto flyer. Paper carries everything with detail.

**Markup:** `class="pwf-navy"` with `data-theme="dark"`, so `ink`, `ink-body`, `ink-muted`, `accent`, `icon` and `line` flip for the navy ground. Add one `.pwf-motif--grid` for the dot-map motif, anchored top-right and bleeding off the corner.

**The consumer provides:** the reversed lockup, an eyebrow, one claim and at most one supporting line.

- Do keep the ground flat. No gradients, glows, tints, photos or patterns as a full background.
- Do put gold on up to three words of the claim (`.pwf-hl`, `gold-light`), or let the motif's gold point be the gold, not both.
- Do set secondary text in `navy-text` (5.8:1), never in a lighter navy.
- Don't put white panels with detail on the navy ground; move that content to paper.
