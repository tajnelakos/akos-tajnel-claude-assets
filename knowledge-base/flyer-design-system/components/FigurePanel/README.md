# FigurePanel

Up to three figures in a white panel, stacked (`.pwf-figures`) or in a row (`.pwf-figures--row`). Each figure is `tri-figure` in navy, a `tri-body` label saying what it measures, and a `source` line saying where it came from.

**The consumer provides:** the figure, its label and its source. Illustrative figures say "illustrative" in the source line.

- Do write the number as the market writes it (68% in English, 68 % in German and French, 2,140 · 2.140 · 2 140 by market; see `wording.md`).
- Do add `.pwf-figure--focus` to the one figure the piece is about: it draws a short gold rule, the panel's one gold mark. Leave it off when the panel or page already has its gold.
- Don't round up, compare to an unnamed baseline, or show a figure without its source line.
