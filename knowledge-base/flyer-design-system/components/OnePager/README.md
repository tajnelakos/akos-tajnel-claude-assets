# OnePager

The A4 portrait leave-behind (210 × 297 mm): one persona, one page, printed or sent as a PDF after a meeting.

**Top to bottom:** plot-strip motif along the top edge (the page's one gold), lockup, eyebrow naming the reader, `page-title` (two lines at most), `page-lead` (up to three lines), a row of three figures (`FigurePanel`, `.pwf-figures--row`), two half-width columns (an `EvidenceStrip` and two `FeaturePanel`s), three steps in a row, and a footer with the `ContactBlock` on the left and the fictional-company line on the right, above a hairline.

**The consumer provides:** the persona, the title and lead, three sourced figures, one signal with its evidence, two regulated-workflow points, three steps and the action.

- Do use `.pwf-evidence--plain` here: the strip's gold bar would be the page's second gold.
- Do keep 12 mm (`space-5`) side margins and leave the motif's 14 mm band clear of type.
- Don't let the page run to a second side; cut a section instead of shrinking type below the scale.
