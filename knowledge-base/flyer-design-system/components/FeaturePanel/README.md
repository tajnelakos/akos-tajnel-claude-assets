# FeaturePanel

A white panel with a hairline on paper that pairs a line icon, a one-line title and two to four lines of copy or up to four bullets. The pillars of the message house live in these.

**Markup:** `.pwf-panel.pwf-feature` › `.pwf-feature__head` (icon + `tri-panel-title`) › `tri-body` paragraph or `ul.tri-body`.

**The consumer provides:** an icon from the Icons group, a title in sentence case and the copy.

- Do stack panels `space-2` apart; a trifold panel holds three.
- Do write the title as the claim ("Bankers act on it"), and the copy as its proof.
- Don't put feature panels on the navy ground; don't add a second icon or a coloured fill.
