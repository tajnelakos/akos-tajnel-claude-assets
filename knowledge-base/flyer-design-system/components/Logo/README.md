# Logo

The Seekridge lockup and mark with an outlined wordmark and transparent ground, ready for print. Files are in the Logos asset group.

**Classes:** `.pwf-logo--lockup` (40 mm wide on A4 and A5 fronts; add `.pwf-logo--lockup-s` for 32 mm on a 100 mm trifold panel) and `.pwf-logo--mark` (10 mm). Inside a `data-theme="dark"` container both switch to the paper-coloured files.

**The consumer provides:** nothing but the placement; the class picks the file for the ground.

- Do place the lockup top-left inside the margin on fronts and on the one-pager; the mark goes on a paper back only when the front isn't seen with it (the back of a one-pager).
- Do keep clear space equal to the mark's height on every side; the mark is never smaller than 10 mm, the lockup never narrower than 32 mm.
- Do use one logo per side.
- Don't recolour, outline, stretch or add effects; don't rebuild the wordmark in live text for print.
