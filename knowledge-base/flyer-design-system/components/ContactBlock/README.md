# ContactBlock

The close of every piece: a QR code on a square white tile, one action that says what happens after the scan, then the web address and email, each after a line arrow. Replaces the pill-shaped URL button: the brand has no pills.

**Markup:** `.pwf-contact` (add `.pwf-contact--stack` to put the QR above the text) › `.pwf-qr` · `.pwf-contact__cta` · `.pwf-contact__line` ×2. On paper, give the tile a hairline outline.

**The consumer provides:** the action ("Scan to book a backtest"), the campaign URL the QR encodes, and the email.

- Do make the QR at least 20 mm with a quiet zone of 4 modules, in Ink on white; test-scan the printed proof.
- Do encode a short campaign URL with a tracking parameter, and print the same URL (without the parameter) beside it.
- Do use one action per piece. "Learn more" is not an action.
- Don't colour the QR navy or gold, put a logo inside it, or set it on navy without its white tile.
- The sample QR in the QR asset group encodes `https://parcelwise.example/demo`. Replace it before print.
