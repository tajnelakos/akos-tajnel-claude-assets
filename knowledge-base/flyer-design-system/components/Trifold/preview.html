The problem

Your next refinance is already in your CRM

Fixed rates end, salaries start landing at another bank and a local lender cuts its two-year rate. The customer notices. Too often, the bank notices after they have moved.

Lists pulled by rate-reset date miss most of these moments, and none of them can say why one customer matters more than another.

“A lead you can’t explain is a liability, not an opportunity.”
