The Parcelwise lockup and mark with an outlined wordmark and transparent ground, ready for print. `pw-lockup-navy.svg` and `pw-mark-navy.svg` (navy strokes, gold lot) go on paper; `pw-lockup-paper.svg` and `pw-mark-paper.svg` (paper strokes, gold-light lot) go on the navy ground. Lockup 40 mm wide on fronts (32 mm on a 100 mm trifold panel), never narrower than 32 mm; mark 10 mm, never smaller. Clear space equals the mark's height. One logo per side. The source files with live text, the palette sheet and the misuse sheet stay in `knowledge-base/branding-guideline/`.
