The Seekridge line icons (24 grid, 1.75 stroke, square caps, no fills) in two colourways: `-navy` for paper and white panels, `-paper` for the navy ground. In print: 6 mm beside a panel title, 9 mm above a benefit, 4 mm in evidence rows and layer chips. The three data layers always use `customer`, `market` and `property`; `calendar`, `pin` and `clock` are for event details. Never fill, recolour, set in a disc or mix with another set.
