How I use AI

Repeatable workflows, not one-off prompts

Everything above comes from workflows I set up in Claude. Each one is a skill: written instructions that first read the same company knowledge, then do one job well.

  1. 1Read the knowledge base

    Positioning, personas, messaging, competitors and brand rules, shared by every skill.

  2. 2Add the input

    A CRM export, call transcripts, a launch brief or a competitor's news.

  3. 3Deliver the finished piece

    A report, a battlecard, a one-pager or a video, already on-brand.

  4. 4Review before it ships

    I read everything. The skill flags what it couldn't confirm, so I know where to look.

Loops that keep it current

The competitor analysis loop

Every 2 months→Research per region→Competitor analysis and battlecards updated

Each round of research updates both the competitor analysis and the battlecards, so what reps say stays current.

The win/loss analysis loop

Call transcripts, win/loss CRM data→Win/loss, call analysis→Win/loss analysis and battlecards refresh

What buyers say, and why deals close, goes straight back into what sales says next.

The monthly self-check

Outdated check runs on the 1st→New findings in the latest win/loss analysis→Positioning doc updated

It keeps the core documents in step with the newest analysis.
