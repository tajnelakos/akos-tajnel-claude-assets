## Input

> Deal context: first meeting with a regional bank's Head of Mortgage Lending. The bank wants to grow and retain its mortgage book and loses customers to brokers at rate reset. No known competitor in the deal yet. Build the deck.

## Stage 1 output: the outline

```
## Deck Outline — Regional bank, first meeting with Head of Mortgage Lending
Persona: Head of Mortgage Lending (lending/growth buyer) · Stage: First substantive meeting

1. Find the peaks in your mortgage book.   (L1 Cover)
   Purpose: Open on their book and the growth it hides, not on Seekridge.
   Key point: Know which customers to call this month, and why, before a competitor does.

2. Most of your mortgage book is quiet.   (L2 Quiet book)
   Purpose: Name the problem in their terms: their systems know the loan, not the customer's next step.
   Key point: The opportunities are already in the book; nobody can see which customers are moving.

3. Four ways your mortgage book grows.   (L3 Four cards)
   Purpose: The promise before the mechanism. This persona buys growth levers.
   Key point: Keep loans at rate reset, win loans held elsewhere, lend for the next step, bring their insurance over.

4. The bank that calls first usually keeps the loan.   (L4 Statement)
   Purpose: Make timing the stake, then ask how they learn today that a customer is leaving.
   Key point: Customers don't announce a refinance.

5. Every week, a few customers rise out of the book.   (L5 Moments on the ridge)
   Purpose: The hero visual: the same book, now with four moments, each paired with the conversation it opens.
   Key point: Each moment is a reason to call, with the evidence attached.

6. Your data, plus the market, in one moment.   (L6 Data layers)
   Purpose: How it works, in one picture: transaction, CRM and market data combine into one moment.
   Key point: One point from each layer makes a call worth making.

7. Every moment shows why, so advisors trust it and risk can sign it off.   (L7 Split with product)
   Purpose: The differentiator, shown in Evidence Trail, framed as advisor adoption for this buyer.
   Key point: No score without a reason.

8. Every moment, matched to a product and an owner.   (L8 Moment → product → owner)
   Purpose: Connect moments to revenue and to their choice of channel.
   Key point: Advisors, a digital campaign or a partner, product by product.

9. Ranked in the CRM your advisors already use.   (L9 Steps and product)
   Purpose: Pre-empt the integration-effort concern this persona carries.
   Key point: Nothing new for advisors to learn.

10. Your mortgage book, working for you.   (L10 Benefits with outcome)
    Purpose: Sum up as three steps and one outcome.
    Key point: Their next mortgage, top-up or insurance stays with you.

11. We prove it on your customers before go-live.   (L11 Figure and plan)
    Purpose: One figure, honestly sourced, then the way to prove it on their own book.
    Key point: The only figure that should convince them is a backtest on their customers.

12. Built for regulated lenders from day one.   (L12 Three trust cards)
    Purpose: Keep risk and IT objections from stalling the deal later; short for this buyer.
    Key point: Data stays in the EU, every moment is logged, consent rules are built in.

13. Already finding moments for lenders across Europe.   (L13 Logo wall)
    Purpose: Social proof from lenders like them.
    Key point: Name the two closest to their market.

14. Let's find the first peaks in your book.   (L14 Close)
    Purpose: A specific next step, agreed in the room.
    Key point: A five-week backtest on a sample of their customers. Lending, risk and IT join.

Appendix: divider, ranked statement (for a numbers-first room), moments on the paper ground, moments forming, three layers into one moment.
```

## Stage 2 output: the slides

Built from the [slide kit](./slide-kit/layouts.md): 14 main slides and 5 in the appendix, each with speaker notes.

What this deck shows about the kit:
- **The ridge carries the story.** Slide 2 shows the book as quiet, slide 5 the same book with moments rising, and appendix slide 18 the moments still forming. It's one illustration, read three ways.
- **The labels have two layers.** The pill says what's happening in the customer's life; the line under it says the conversation it opens for the bank.
- **One gold element per slide.** The urgent moment, the first lever, the digital-campaign owner, the outcome card.
- **Placeholders the rep fills in:** `[Bank]` and `[Month 2026]` on the cover, `[Rep name] · [email]` on the close.

Compare with [`sample-deck-outline.md`](./sample-deck-outline.md): same product, a Head of Retail Banking in the room, and a different order. That deck leads with what customers buy elsewhere and the advisor-adoption objection, not refinancing volume.
