02 · The challenge · quiet book

The challenge

Most of your mortgage book is quiet.

Your systems know the loan: balance, rate and when the fixed term ends. They don't know who's about to refinance elsewhere, buy, renovate or borrow more.

04 · Why timing · statement

Why timing matters

The bank that calls first usually keeps the loan.

Customers don't announce a refinance. By the time they ask for a redemption statement, a broker has already found them a rate.

Seekridge04
One line, said slowly. Then ask how they find out today that a customer is about to leave.
