The challenge

Most of your customer base looks quiet.

Your systems know the account: balance, products and last contact. They don't know whose salary lands elsewhere, who is moving money out, or who is ready for a mortgage.

04 · Why timing · statement

Why timing matters

The bank that calls first usually keeps the customer.

Customers don't announce that they're moving their money. By the time the balance drops, another bank has already made its offer.

Seekridge04
One line, said slowly. Then ask how they find out today that a customer is moving money out.

12 · Security and compliance · three cards

Security and compliance

Built for regulated banks from day one.

Your data stays in the EU

Transactions are pseudonymised, hosted in the EU and encrypted at rest and in transit. Never used to train models for anyone else.

An audit log for every moment

What each moment said, who saw it and what was done with it, kept for review.

Consent rules built in

Transaction data is used only for the purposes you approve, and only customers who can be contacted for an offer are ranked, channel by channel.
