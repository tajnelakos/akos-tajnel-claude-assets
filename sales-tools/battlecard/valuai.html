Their pitch, and the quick dismiss

They say

“AI-native customer moments, with regulatory explanations. Now generally available.”

Quick dismiss

It shipped, but the explanation is a chart of which feature categories matter across the model, not the property, market and customer data points behind one customer. One named reference, no proof at scale, and buyers report it thins out on commercial and mixed-use property.

They show what the model weighs. We show why this customer, on the buyer’s own data, live.

Where they’re strong · what to say

  • A strong first demo and a modern UI
    Don’t fight the demo. Move the bar: “Ask them to run one moment live, on a customer your team picks.”
  • Live CRM integration, same as ours
    Treat it as parity. Don’t spend time on it; spend it on the evidence trail.
  • Now cheaper than us, ~9% on average
    Don’t discount. Price the cost of an explanation that fails an audit, not the licence.

Where they’re weak · how to use it

  • Explanation is model-level, not customer-level
    Ask for a customer-level breakdown in the second meeting. Buyers who pushed found the gap themselves.
  • Thin on commercial and mixed-use property
    If the book has business customers, run the live test on those records specifically.
  • Delivery has lagged the pitch
    One customer reports a longer implementation than promised. Ask for references on timelines, not just the product.

Where we’re weaker · how to handle it

  • We’re the smaller company
    Answer with specifics, not reassurance: data-portability terms, number of similar-sized customers live, runway if they ask.
  • No on-prem deployment
    ValuAI is cloud-only too, so it’s rarely the decider here. Raise it early if the bank has an on-prem rule.

Landmines to lay

  1. “How many customers run the explanation feature live today, not just have access to it?”Surfaces the one-reference problem without you saying it.
  2. “Could your team pick five customers, and have each vendor show why each one was flagged?”Moves the evaluation from a demo to a live test, where we win.
  3. “How much of your book is commercial or mixed-use property?”Sets up the property types where their moments are weakest.

What buyers told us

Don’t say

  • “It’s still in beta” or “it doesn’t exist”. It shipped in July 2026, and buyers will check.
  • “AI-native is just marketing.” It’s a real approach; it just isn’t the differentiator any more.
  • “Their product isn’t ready.” They won 7 of our last 8 head-to-heads. Compete on proof, not on dismissal.
