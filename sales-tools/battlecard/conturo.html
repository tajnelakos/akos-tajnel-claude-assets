Their pitch, and the quick dismiss

They say

“Two decades of trusted banking analytics that banks across the region rely on.”

Quick dismiss

The relationship and the data are real. The experience is not: a dated interface, customer data loaded by batch file, slow support, and an insights report that gives every customer the same methodology PDF.

They give you a score and a PDF. We show why this customer, inside your bankers’ CRM.

Where they’re strong, and what to say

  • A decade of relationships
    Respect it. Don’t pitch a switch; ask what has changed since they signed, in their needs and in what Conturo shipped.
  • Largest historical dataset in the region
    Concede the depth. Ask whether more history matters more than moments bankers act on today.
  • On-prem deployment
    Ask early. If on-prem is a hard rule, we will lose. Find out in the first call, not the last.

Where they’re weak, and how to use it

  • No live CRM integration yet
    Ask how moments reach bankers today. Buyers describe exports and manual lists.
  • Slow support and releases
    Let the buyer name it: ask how long their last support ticket took.
  • The same explanation for every customer
    Show a customer-level evidence trail next to their methodology PDF.

Where we’re weaker, and how to handle it

  • No on-prem option
    Raise it ourselves when residency comes up. It is a product gap, not a talk track.
  • A younger vendor
    Answer with references and a pilot, not with reassurance.

Landmines to lay

  1. “How long did your last support ticket with your current vendor take to resolve?”Surfaces the pain without criticising their choice.
  2. “When an auditor asks why one customer was on a list, what does the report show?”Brings out the same-PDF-for-everyone problem.
  3. “Is on-premises a firm requirement, or a preference?”Qualifies the one deal-breaker early.

What buyers told us

Don’t say

  • “Conturo is outdated.” It reads as an attack on the buyer’s own choice. Name a specific pain instead.
  • “Their data is worse.” The documented weakness is interface, support and releases, not data quality.
  • “They have no CRM integration.” A connector is in pilot; say “not yet available”.
