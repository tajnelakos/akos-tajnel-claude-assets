SeekridgevsPocketlens

How to sell against Pocketlens: what they claim, what is actually true, and the questions that win the deal.

Threat LowUpdated September 2026Owner PMM, competitive intelSource competitive-landscape.md
Kept intentionally brief — Pocketlens does a different, customer-facing job and is rarely a genuine head-to-head competitor. See competitive-landscape.md's note on priority before investing more here.
Company Overview & Strategy
Pocketlens sells a money-insights widget that banks embed in their app: spending summaries and tips for the customer. It comes up mostly at credit unions and when a bank's digital channel team runs its own evaluation — not as a head-to-head competitor for bank-facing moments.
Positioning Framework
They say
"Money insights your customers will love, inside your banking app."
Reality
Built for customers, not for bankers — nothing reaches the relationship manager or the CRM, no ranked opportunities, and no evidence trail a risk team can review.
We win because
Insights for customers and opportunities for bankers are different jobs. Pocketlens can stay in the app; Seekridge tells your bankers who to call and why.
Value Pillars & Differentiators
01
Bank-facing by design
Moments go to the banker who owns the relationship, inside the CRM.
02
Compliance and audit capability by default
Every moment logged, with its evidence, for risk and data protection.
Discovery Question
When a customer opens an insight in the app, does anyone at the bank see it — and does it ever turn into a conversation?
Objection Handler
"We already have Pocketlens in our app."
That's a different problem than the one we solve — no need to replace it if your customers like it. The question is whether your bankers know which customers to call this week, and why.

Do-Not-Say List

  • Don't disparage Pocketlens's customer-facing product — we don't compete there, and doing so reads as reaching for a fight that isn't ours.
