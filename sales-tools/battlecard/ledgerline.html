Their pitch, and the quick dismiss

They say

“The fastest, cheapest transaction data in the market. Now audit-ready.”

Quick dismiss

The data is fast and cheap, and the audit log is real. But it logs API calls, not why a customer was chosen. There is still no customer-data layer and no explained moment: the buyer’s team builds the targeting, and has to defend it.

They sell data your team turns into decisions. We deliver the decision, with its evidence.

Where they’re strong, and what to say

  • Lowest price per call
    Don’t price-match. Price the build: the data team’s time to turn a feed into moments, and to explain them to risk.
  • Self-serve, live in minutes
    Agree, then redefine “live”. “The feed is instant. When are moments in your bankers’ CRM, validated on your data?”
  • Growing enterprise story
    Treat the audit log as real. Then ask what it records: API calls, or the reason behind each customer.

Where they’re weak, and how to use it

  • No customer-data layer or moments
    Ask who will build and maintain the targeting logic. Usually it’s a data team that already has a backlog.
  • Explainability is the buyer’s job
    Give the buyer the review pack early, so model risk sees the method before procurement compares prices. Ledgerline deals we lose stay with procurement and the data team.
  • Flow Alerts is still a beta
    It flags a transfer, without a ranking or a reason. Ask who decides which alerts a banker should act on, and how that is explained.

Where we’re weaker, and how to handle it

  • We cost more per customer
    Never discount to close the gap. Offer a side-by-side on one real customer segment instead.
  • Slower to switch on
    Be honest: weeks, not minutes. Show the four-step plan from connection to bankers using it.

Landmines to lay

  1. “If an auditor asks why one customer was contacted, what would your team show them?”Moves the deal from price to evidence, where we win.
  2. “Who will build the moments on top of the data feed, and who maintains them?”Makes the hidden build cost visible.
  3. “Has Ledgerline shown you how you’d explain one targeting decision, or only price and speed?”A line that worked in a live call; let the buyer find the gap.

What buyers told us

Don’t say

  • “Their data is inaccurate.” There’s no evidence for it, and it is easy to get caught overstating.
  • “They have no audit log.” Ledgerline Enterprise has one. Say what it doesn’t log.
  • “We can match their price.” Reframe to the cost of building and defending moments yourself.
