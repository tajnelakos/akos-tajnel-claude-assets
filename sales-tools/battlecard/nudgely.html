SeekridgevsNudgely

In-app money insights for the bank’s customers. Rarely in our lending deals, but a real rival at small and digital-first banks, where it is cheap, familiar and already installed.

Their pitch, and the quick dismiss

They say

“Money insights your customers actually open.”

Quick dismiss

Customers open them, for a while. But nothing reaches an advisor: buyers tell us the insights changed no conversations. It has no evidence trail, and it isn’t built for a bank’s lending or risk team.

Nudgely sends customers a tip in the app. We give advisors the reason to call, in the tools they already use.

Where they’re strong, and what to say

  • Already installed and familiar
    Don’t attack it. Suggest running both: insights in the app, moments for advisors.
  • Simple per-user pricing
    Match on clarity, not price. Show the cost per appointment booked, not per user.

Where they’re weak, and how to use it

  • Engagement fades after the first weeks
    Offer a pilot measured on advisor appointments, the outcome buyers name first.
  • Nothing for advisors or risk
    For a bank with an advisor network, moments with evidence reach the customers the app never will.

Where we’re weaker, and how to handle it

  • No customer-facing app widget
    Don’t compete on the app. Compete on advisor adoption and on the reason behind each moment.

Landmines to lay

  1. “How many advisor conversations did the in-app insights lead to last quarter?”Brings out the gap buyers already feel.
  2. “Does your lending team see anything from the app?”Opens the advisor conversation.
  3. “What would make a new tool stick with advisors this time?”Lets the buyer describe the pilot we should run.

What buyers told us

Don’t say

  • “Nudgely is a toy.” Digital teams like it. We win on advisor adoption, not on disparaging it.
  • “It’s free anyway.” No longer true: the free plan became a 14-day trial in October 2026.
