Product marketing · Launch process

From shaping the product to measuring it, in five stages

One messaging master feeds every asset. Demos are checked per market before sales shows them. Success is defined before launch, so feedback measures against a target.

The flow

Each stage produces what the next one needs

The order is fixed; the timing isn't. A new product launch and a feature release move at different speeds, but neither skips a stage.

Before stage 1

A one-page launch brief. What changes and for whom, the launch size (which sets the launch kit), who it’s for, the story, the name, commercials and timing, and who decides. See the template.

01

Define

Settle what the product is, what it's called and what it costs before a single asset is made.

  • Shape the product. Critique features with product and ground them in real use cases for retail banks, savings banks and credit unions.
  • Write the messaging master. One source document sets the tagline, core premise, stage headlines, outcomes and the main illustration that every asset reuses.positioning.md
  • Name and position. Lock solution and feature names and apply the copy rules.writing.md
  • Package and price. Set up the platform pricing, the Base, Ridge and Summit web-app tiers, market price lists and the CRM, plus an upsell path for existing customers: which upgrade or add-on the launch creates for them.
02

Prove

Find out what works with real customers and real data, and agree what success looks like.

  • Early product pilot. Test the product with 1–2 existing customers before launch.
  • Set launch KPIs. Agree targets with product and sales, for example adoption, demo requests and pipeline, so feedback is measured against them.
  • Build and check market demos. Build a demo for each market and segment with local data, language and relevant moments, then rate each one, so we know what sales can show and claim before they use it.

03

Enable

Everyone who talks to a customer hears about the launch from us first.

  • Enable sales. Quick reference doc, enablement deck, demo playbook and market training.sales-deck-builderbattlecards
  • Enable CSMs and support. What changes for existing customers, a product FAQ and how to handle first questions and issues.
04

Launch

Every channel reuses the messaging master, so the web page, press release and carousel say the same thing.

  • Update the website. Segment and solution pages with 30-second videos, mocked up and then built in Framer.video-design-system
  • Press release and partner co-marketing. External announcement of the launch, plus joint announcements and content with partners.
  • Launch to users. In-app notifications (Appcues), email, blog post and LinkedIn carousel.emailblogcarousels
  • Events. Event-specific video, flyer, roll-up and a refreshed stand panel.flyers
05

Learn

Feedback, measured against the launch KPIs set in stage 2.

First 90 days

Early signs of traction

  • Demo bug checks and demo improvements
  • Feature adoption and Appcues engagement
  • Campaign performance: email, LinkedIn, webpage traffic
Ongoing

Business results

  • Sales feedback and objections that come up on calls (call analysis)
  • Win/loss rates (win/loss)
  • Website demo requests and pipeline influenced
  • Customer and CSM feedback on the quality of the product's outcomes

Every result is read against a target agreed before launch, then fed back into shaping the next release.

Seekridge is a fictional company. The process is the one I run in real launches; the readiness ratings are illustrative.
