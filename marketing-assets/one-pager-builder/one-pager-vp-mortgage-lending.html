One customer, one rate reset

Months of warning, not a redemption letter.

Today the first sign a customer is leaving is the paperwork from their new bank. Seekridge moves that moment forward, to when an advisor can still do something about it.

What’s inside

  • Rate-reset moments

    Customers likely to refinance elsewhere, months before the fixed period ends.

  • Top-up moments

    Homeowners with the equity for a top-up or renovation loan.

  • Moving moments

    Customers likely to move house and need their next mortgage.

  • An evidence trail

    The property, market and customer data behind each moment, and its weight.

  • CRM sync

    Moments land as tasks in the advisor queues you already run.

  • Backtests

    Every model checked against your own past outcomes before go-live.
