Refinancing elsewhere

Win the mortgages held at other banks

For advisors
Call before the fixed rate ends at the other bank, and offer to move the loan.
How we spot it
Monthly payments to another lender, and when the fixed period is likely to end.
Buying a home

Be the first bank they ask for a mortgage

For advisors
Reach customers while they are still searching, before a broker does.
How we spot it
Savings growth, life stage, and homes in their price range coming up nearby.
Renovating

Fund the renovation before work starts

For advisors
Offer a renovation or green loan while the work is still being planned.
How we spot it
The home’s age and energy rating, the equity in it, and renovation permits on the street.
New home to insure

Insure the new home from the first day

For advisors
Offer home insurance as soon as a customer takes on a property.
How we spot it
A purchase payment from their account, and a new land-register entry in their name.

Call before their bank does
