## Input

> Build a one-pager for retail banking leaders at banks. Topic: five moments — customers moving savings out, customers getting ready for a mortgage, salaries landing at another bank, a family growing, and customers saving for a car.

## Output

**Positioning angle used:** positioning.md's Retail banking row ("Know which customers to call this month, and why — main-bank and growth moments — mortgage-ready, car purchase goal, family growing — before a competitor sees them"), widened to the five moments in knowledge-base.md.

## Why it's built this way

- **One audience, one promise.** Every block answers the same question for a retail banking leader: which customers to call, and why, before another bank does.
- **Value first.** Each moment card leads with the business outcome, then what changes for bankers, then the data that makes it possible, so the page reads in the buyer's language.
- **Show the evidence, not a dashboard.** Instead of a full-screen product screenshot, each card shows the three data points behind its moment, and the back shows one moment as a banker would see it.
- **The rollout is a trust trigger, not filler.** This persona has been burned by "instant" claims; four named steps that separate going live from bankers using it answer that directly.
