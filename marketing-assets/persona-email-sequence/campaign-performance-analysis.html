Key findings

  • 14 vs 8 · 19 vs 9Specific subject lines won, but “specific” differs by persona

    Risk buyers: their fear as a question. Mortgage buyers: a hard number.

  • 6 · 8 repliesEmail 4 reversed the drop in both sequences

    Up from 4 and 5. It’s the trust-trigger email: peer references, or a real rollout plan.

  • 2.30% vs 1.92%Mortgage leaders booked more meetings

    As expected: risk buyers are sceptical by job, whatever the copy.

  • 0 unsubscribesThe soft close cost nothing

    No “last chance” email needed to end either sequence.
