# Sample output

Sequence for the Head of Retail Banking persona ([`personas.md`](../../knowledge-base/personas.md)).

---

## Input

> Persona: Head of Retail Banking. Trigger: downloaded our "Products per customer: what your transaction data already knows" guide from the site.

## Output

```
## Email Sequence — Head of Retail Banking — Trigger: guide download

### Email 1 — "Where your customers buy what you could sell them" — Day 0
You downloaded our guide on products per customer, so you've probably seen the pattern in your own data: a car loan with you, the insurance somewhere else the same week; a renovation financed by another lender.
If you'd like to see how many of those moments sit in a sample of your own customers right now, it takes about 15 minutes, on your data, not a demo.
Decision criterion this advances: evidence on their own customers (top-ranked criterion) — moves from the guide's general argument to a concrete look at their base.

### Email 2 — "Would your advisors believe this list?" — Day 4
The question that decides whether an opportunity list turns into business: will an advisor believe it enough to call?
Every moment shows the data points behind it and how much each counted, and we backtest the moments on your own past customers before go-live. Worth a look if you're comparing vendors on more than a headline hit rate.
Decision criterion this advances: advisor adoption (2nd-ranked criterion), through the evidence that makes a moment believable.

### Email 3 — "What changes for advisors, and what doesn't" — Day 9
Last thing before I stop filling your inbox: moments arrive as tasks in the CRM your advisors already use, a short list each week, with a suggested next step. No new dashboard.
Happy to walk through what that would look like for your setup, no pressure if the timing isn't right.
Decision criterion this advances: fit with how each product is sold, and integration effort — closes on the remaining concerns without urgency tactics.
```

Note: no 4th email pushing "last chance" urgency — this persona is described in `personas.md` as wary of tools and pitches that add work for advisors, so the sequence ends on a low-pressure, real-effort answer instead.
